Delete your Yormio account

You can initiate account deletion inside Yormio. If you no longer have the app or cannot access the in-app flow, you can submit a deletion request here.

Subscription reminder: deleting your Yormio account does not cancel an Apple App Store or Google Play subscription. Manage or cancel the subscription separately in the store that billed you.

Option 1: delete in the app

If you still have access to Yormio, use the account deletion action in the app’s account/settings area. The current in-app deletion flow requests deletion of the authenticated Supabase account, clears the active local account database and signs out the local session.

Option 2: request deletion on the web

Use the form below if you cannot use the in-app flow. The request must contain enough information for us to locate and verify the account. We may contact you for identity verification before deleting data. We will never ask for your password.

What deletion covers

After successful verification, deletion is intended to remove the Yormio account and associated Yormio-controlled cloud data that is not required to be retained, including synced reminders/lists and owner-linked share records. Local data on a device you still possess should also be removed through the in-app deletion flow or by removing the app’s storage.

Some records may be retained where reasonably necessary for security, fraud prevention, legal obligations, dispute handling, or where independent providers such as Apple, Google, RevenueCat or infrastructure providers maintain records under their own obligations and policies.
